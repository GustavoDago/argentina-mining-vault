# Daily Report - August 10, 2026

## 1. Vaca Muerta: Production Records & M&A Activity
* **Production Milestone:** Argentina achieved a historic oil production record of 914,900 barrels per day (bpd) in June 2026, representing a 17.1% YoY increase. This growth is predominantly driven by non-conventional production from Vaca Muerta, bringing the country closer to the strategic goal of 1 million bpd.
* **Infrastructure Challenge:** While production is booming, reports indicate that the next critical step is expanding infrastructure to convert this growth into increased export volumes.
* **Fracturing Activity:** Despite a slight slowdown in July, Vaca Muerta recorded nearly 17,000 hydraulic fractures year-to-date, marking an 18% increase compared to 2025. The basin is on track to set another annual record in 2026.
* **M&A Surge:** The energy sector, propelled by Vaca Muerta, led Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A) in Argentina during H1 2026. Total M&A activity increased by 38%, reaching up to US$ 5.7 billion, demonstrating strong investor confidence in the region.
