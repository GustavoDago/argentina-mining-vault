## Vaca Muerta & Hidrocarburos
*   **Vaca Muerta Oil Sur (VMOS) Pipeline Completion:** Techint-Sacde successfully completed the construction of the Vaca Muerta Oil Sur (VMOS) pipeline after 15 months of work. Furthermore, manufacturing of the offshore duct commenced in Río Negro.
*   **Production & M&A:** Argentina achieved a historic oil production record of 914,900 bpd in June 2026 (+17.1% YoY). M&A activity in Vaca Muerta reached US$ 5.7 billion in H1 2026.
*   **Investment Spikes:** Tecpetrol's US$ 6.4 billion upstream investment in Los Toldos II Este and Compañía Mega's US$ 365 million midstream gas expansion were officially approved under the RIGI framework. Continental Resources projected a US$ 4 billion investment in Vaca Muerta following offers for 6 of 8 new hydrocarbon blocks tendered by GyP in Neuquén. GeoPark requested RIGI entry for a >US$ 1 billion investment (Loma Jarillosa Este and Puesto Silva Oeste) aiming to increase production to 20,000 bpd in three years. YPF submitted a US$ 51 billion Argentina LNG project to RIGI.
*   **Resource Base:** Vaca Muerta doubled its recoverable resources to nearly 30 billion barrels and surpassed 40 active drilling rigs. Energy exports doubled, reaching a USD 900M trade surplus.
*   **Palermo Aike:** YPF/CGC set a date for the second shale well in Palermo Aike.

## Cobre (Copper)
*   **San Jorge (Mendoza):** The government approved the entry of PSJ Cobre Mendocino (San Jorge project) into the RIGI framework with an investment of US$ 891 million.
*   **Distrito Vicuña (San Juan):** Officially approved into the RIGI framework under the PEELP category, confirming a US$ 9.737 million investment plan. A San Juan-Vicuña infrastructure agreement was signed for US$ 250M.
*   **Taca Taca (Salta):** Active exploration campaigns advanced to secure critical resources (water), map routes, and lay high-voltage lines in Salta.
*   **Logistics Bottlenecks:** The mining boom created severe logistical constraints, notably a critical shortage of trained high-mountain truck drivers in San Juan, affecting Los Azules and Josemaría.

## Litio (Lithium)
*   **RIGI Approvals:** Rio Tinto's Rincón project (US$ 2.500M) and Arcadium Lithium's Fénix expansion (US$ 251M) were formally approved under the RIGI framework. The government also approved RIGI incentives for lithium mine expansions with ties to China. Cauchari-Olaroz Phase 2 secured US$ 220M financing. Eramet Centenario Ratones was mentioned in recent operations.
*   **Milestones:** Ganfeng Lithium completed its first lithium export from Salta.
*   **Regulatory Frictions (NOA):** Growing tension emerged in the NOA region (Salta, Jujuy, Catamarca) between national RIGI benefits and the provincial REPEM law, which mandates local hiring quotas. Additionally, Río Negro paralyzed the Calcatreu gold/silver project due to non-compliance with the provincial 80% local hiring quota (80/20 rule), highlighting operational risks related to social license.
*   **Logistics:** Increasing reliance on the upcoming Belgrano Cargas railway privatization for NOA lithium export routes.

## RIGI (Régimen de Incentivo para Grandes Inversiones)
*   **Cumulative Milestone:** Total announced investments under the RIGI framework surpassed the historical threshold of US$ 100 billion. The framework now includes 36 major projects (approaching US$ 94.9B before the latest wave of approvals pushed it over US$ 100B).

## Corredor Bioceánico & Logística
*   **Transandean Rail Tunnel:** A private consortium proposed a US$ 9.6 billion rail tunnel through the Andes.
*   **Road Corridors:** Chile presented a plan for a bioceanic road corridor. A Corredor Bioceanico Capricornio forum was held.

## Macro Projections
*   **Combined Exports:** Vaca Muerta and the mining sector are jointly projected to generate US$ 51.000 million in combined exports. Goldman Sachs projected a US$ 60 billion value for Vaca Muerta.
*   **Governance:** Catamarca reformed its state mining company, CAMYEN, to modernize its structure and align with other provincial state mining entities.
