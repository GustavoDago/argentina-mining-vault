# Energon Daily Raw News - 2026-08-10

## Vaca Muerta
* According to RICSA (Regional Investment Consulting), Argentina reached a historic oil production record in June 2026, driven by Vaca Muerta. National production reached 914,900 barrels per day (bpd), a 17.1% YoY increase. Although export volumes decreased slightly, higher international crude prices boosted energy export revenues. Non-conventional production in Vaca Muerta remains the main driver, pushing Argentina closer to the 1 million bpd goal. [Source: Diario Río Negro]
* BBVA Research highlighted the historical production record and the leap of shale oil in Vaca Muerta, noting that the challenge now is to expand infrastructure to transform growth into more exports. [Source: El Economista / Ground News]
* Hydraulic fracturing activity in Vaca Muerta slowed slightly in July 2026 after a record first semester but remains among the highest recorded, with nearly 17,000 fractures year-to-date. The activity grew more than 18% in 2026 compared to 2025, retaining the possibility of closing the year with a new record. [Source: rionegro.com.ar / Ground News]
* The energy sector (specifically Vaca Muerta) led Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A) in Argentina during the first semester of 2026. M&A in Argentina increased 38% to reach $4,672 million (or US$ 5.7 billion according to another source). [Source: la Nacion, BorderPeriodismo / Ground News]

## Litio (Lithium)
* Lithium Argentina (operating the Cauchari-Olaroz lithium brine operation in Jujuy in partnership with Ganfeng) announced it will release its Second Quarter 2026 results on August 11, 2026. A recent milestone noted was the August 05, 2026 announcement that Lithium Argentina closed $220 Million of New Debt Facilities at Cauchari-Olaroz. Additionally, they received RIGI Approval for Cauchari-Olaroz Stage 2 Expansion on May 14, 2026. [Source: Junior Mining Network]
* Mining in Argentina grew by 11.4% YoY, driven primarily by lithium and salt. [Source: Diario Río Negro]
