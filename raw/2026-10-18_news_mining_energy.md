# Raw News Ingestion - October 18, 2026

## Vaca Muerta
**Source:** vacamuertaonline.com.ar / econojournal.com.ar
- Vaca Muerta Oil Sur (VMOS) begins loading the first crude export shipment from the new offshore terminal in Río Negro. This confirms the operational success of the pipeline completed earlier by Techint-Sacde.
- YPF signs final engineering and construction (FEED) contracts for the US$ 51 billion LNG project under the RIGI framework, moving the mega-project closer to the Final Investment Decision (FID) expected by early 2027.
- Continental Resources breaks site preparation records for its first six blocks in Vaca Muerta, leveraging the US$ 4 billion RIGI commitment.

## Cobre (Taca Taca, Los Azules, Josemaría)
**Source:** camaraminerasj.com.ar / panorama-minero.com/es / rumbominero.com
- **San Juan (Los Azules / Josemaría):** The task force established to address the critical shortage of high-mountain truck drivers in San Juan announces its first graduating class of 500 specialized logistics operators. However, McEwen Copper and Lundin Mining warn this covers only 20% of the required workforce as both projects ramp up pre-construction and early works.
- **Taca Taca (Salta):** Public hearings for the environmental baseline of the Taca Taca project conclude successfully. First Quantum Minerals prepares to mobilize heavy equipment to the Puna region following their US$ 4.2 billion RIGI commitment.

## Litio (Catamarca, Salta, Jujuy)
**Source:** mineriaenergia.com / discoveryalert.com.au
- **Catamarca:** LIEX S.A. completes the first phase of its US$ 709 million RIGI-approved expansion at the Tres Quebradas (3Q) project. However, tension continues between RIGI benefits and the provincial REPEM law local hiring quotas.
- **Salta:** Posco announces that Phase 2 of its Hombre Muerto Norte expansion is now 50% complete, significantly ahead of schedule, driving further demand for NOA logistics.
- **Jujuy:** Ganfeng Lithium dispatches its largest export cargo to date from the Caucharí-Olaroz project, utilizing newly upgraded rail logistics to the Chilean border.

## RIGI
**Source:** Boletín Oficial de la República Argentina
- Official data confirms that total investments processed and approved under the RIGI framework have now surpassed the **US$ 108 billion** mark, cementing its role as the primary catalyst for the Argentine macroeconomy.

## Corredor Bioceánico de Capricornio
**Source:** argenports.com
- The Inter-American Development Bank (IDB) and private consortia pledge US$ 2.5 billion towards the pre-feasibility and initial earthworks of the new transandean rail tunnel, an essential piece of infrastructure to alleviate the trucking bottleneck for NOA lithium and copper exports.
